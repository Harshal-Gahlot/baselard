- The projects archive includes titles and credits listed on the public Originals page. It does not invent dates or counts.
- Client/label references are shown as text credits, not official logos or endorsements.
- `Featured in` links reflect the linked publications displayed on the founder page. They are not presented as paid endorsements.
- Testimonials retain public-site wording and names.
- The earlier planning brief contained additional metrics, clients, gear and biography detail. Those items are not all independently verifiable on the current public pages; they have not been silently treated as verified facts in this build.
- The existing website's image files remain hosted by the source website. Confirm permission and migrate to owned/local assets before final deployment.
